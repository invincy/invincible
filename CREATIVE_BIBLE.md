# Invincible creative bible

Use with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [permanent Map Editor production standard](docs/map-editor-production-standard.md). This captures established project direction, not automatic access to ChatGPT memory. A film's current brief overrides aesthetic defaults; record new durable preferences here only when established by the user.

## Editorial identity

Make documentary-style historical, geographic and science explainers. The viewer should understand where something happened, how places relate and why a process matters. Established interests include Panipat, the Siliguri Corridor, Gaganyaan and Himalayan earthquakes; these are topic references, not reusable factual claims, fixed scene lists or universal overlays.

Build a clear sequence: establish the location or question, reveal the relevant relationship/process, then show its consequence. Use the approved script and actual voiceover as the structure. Every camera move, route, highlight and asset entrance should explain a spoken idea. Give the viewer time to read geography; avoid arbitrary cinematic motion, repetitive orbiting, bouncing icons and effects on every word.

History needs dated sources and honest uncertainty. Geography needs precise locations and scale. Science needs correct units, dates and a clear distinction between measured observations, models and illustrative diagrams. Do not add unsupported statistics or make an illustration look like verified evidence. Do not invent details to complete a visually pleasing shot.

## Visual identity

- Default new shorts to portrait 9:16, normally 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Preserve an existing project's format unless the user requests a change.
- Aim for premium documentary restraint: real terrain/imagery, warm white text, deep navy or the existing dark documentary card colour, and selective muted gold accents. Keep natural terrain colours; use semantic river/border/highlight colours when they communicate meaning. Do not recolour all geography gold or apply this video palette to unrelated dashboard/LIC pages.
- Reuse versioned presentation settings in `creator/map-defaults.js` and [Documentary Terrain](docs/documentary-terrain.md). Saved maps retain their settings; a new style default must not rewrite old projects. Do not introduce a parallel CSS/JSON theme system.
- Use the existing locally bundled Latin/Devanagari fonts through `creator/map-fonts.js`. Check Hindi shaping, long place names, contrast and output-size legibility. Prefer a coherent family, clear weight hierarchy, generous spacing and restrained rounded cards or local halos over decorative font mixing.
- Follow the permanent standard's output-scaled label targets (key locations 48–64 px at a 1080-pixel short edge). Start from the actual saved preset and verify encoded output; do not shrink labels to squeeze in extra information. Keep labels near geographic anchors, clear of routes, borders and faces.
- Use the existing presentation safe areas for platform controls and captions if requested. Inspect at full resolution and phone size, including moving transitions. Keep the map's focal point legible in the narrow portrait composition.

Default screen text is essential geographic labels. Titles, dates, statistics, fact cards and narration captions require the current brief's authorization. Never add transcript subtitles automatically. Keep required source attribution. Use concise editor controls and actionable messages; keep production reasoning outside the viewer's picture.

## Motion and imagery

North-up is the geographic default. Establish an anchor before moving; arrive at a named location by its spoken cue, hold for comprehension and ease into the next relationship. Reveal travel progressively and preserve the actual direction. A comparison layout does not prove geographic deployment.

Reuse existing project and shared-library assets before generating or downloading alternatives. Check crop, alpha, facing, resolution, rights and historical status. Country-flag textures are artwork, not boundary evidence. A schematic battlefield, generated portrait or science illustration must not be presented as a measured reconstruction.

## Creative acceptance

A successful passage answers the narration visually, uses accurate geography, remains readable on a phone and moves purposefully without clutter. Review with the real recording. Report uncertain sources, provisional cues, missing assets and unreviewed passages; polished appearance alone is not approval.
