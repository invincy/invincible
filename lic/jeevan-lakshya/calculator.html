YOUR PLAN

GUARANTEED AND NON-GUARANTEED

Keep the promise and the projection separate.

Survival maturity: Basic Sum Assured plus vested Simple Reversionary Bonuses and Final Additional Bonus, if any. Future bonuses are not guaranteed; once declared, reversionary bonuses vest on LIC's terms. They are simple bonuses, not compound interest.

Optional sensitivity inputs, initially zero. The annual assumption is applied over the whole policy term, including after an in-force death. These are user assumptions, not LIC-declared rates or LIC's 4%/8% investment-return illustrations.

POLICY STRUCTURE

Entry: 18–50 years; term: 13–25 years; maturity age: 31–65. Minimum entry age uses last birthday; maximum entry and maturity ages use nearer birthday. LIC must confirm the age basis and underwriting.

PREMIUM EVIDENCE

Only exact ₹2 lakh brochure samples are shown. Other ages, terms, Sum Assured values, payment modes and riders require an LIC quotation. High Sum Assured and mode rebates make simple scaling unreliable. Tax is subject to applicable rules; no tax exemption is promised.

CASH FLOW

Premiums appear in the policy year they are due at the start; income and maturity appear at the anniversary ending that year. Death is assumed just before the selected anniversary. On an exact anniversary, income starts on that anniversary; otherwise it starts on the following anniversary, ending before maturity.
