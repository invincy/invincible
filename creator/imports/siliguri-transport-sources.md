## Expanded regional / editorial map revision

The wider connection covers Purnea/Katihar–Siliguri–Guwahati. The live Overpass service failed (502/rate limiting/timeouts), so the expanded view uses published Natural Earth road and railway data, not invented connectors. Two continuous source-network paths through the Siliguri area are derived using source vertices and existing geometric intersections, clipped to Indian territory and the regional extent, then lightly simplified for display. Road numbering and current operational status are not inferred; captions say regional road/rail connections rather than claiming a specific NH number. These are generalized regional reference alignments, not navigation data. The original detailed corridor OSM catalog is retained separately as prior source material.
